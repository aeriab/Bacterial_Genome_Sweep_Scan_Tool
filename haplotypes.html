What these show

Each image is one CNN input window: 120 C. difficile genomes (rows) × 400 polymorphic sites (columns), the same window width and sample count used for the Clostridioides difficile strain filtered scan in the tool. Before a window is built, genomes are drawn with a cap of one genome per whole-genome relatedness cluster (<1% pairwise divergence), and the haplotype rows are clustered on minor alleles at ≥20% frequency with a missing-data-aware distance. Rows are ordered by similarity, so shared haplotype backgrounds appear as horizontal bands.
